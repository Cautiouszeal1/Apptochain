02 / CONTEXT

NFT Pulse — what to know right now

The forces shaping NFT markets today, in plain terms.

STANDARDS

ERC-721 vs ERC-1155

Most PFP and 1-of-1 art collections still run on ERC-721. ERC-1155 has taken over gaming assets and semi-fungible items, since one contract can hold many token types at once — cheaper to mint, cheaper to move.

LIQUIDITY

Floor price ≠ real liquidity

A listed floor price only tells you the cheapest ask — not what actually clears. Depth of bids and recent sold volume matter more than the headline number when judging a collection's health.

UTILITY

Utility-first collections are winning

Pure speculation cycles have cooled. Collections tied to real access — gated communities, in-game assets, staking rights, or on-chain identity — are holding attention longer than art-only drops.

CROSS-CHAIN

NFTs are going multi-chain

Ethereum still leads in blue-chip value, but Solana, Base, and BNB Chain have pulled meaningful mint volume thanks to lower fees — which is exactly the kind of activity AppToChain is built to surface.

TOOLING

Wallets are becoming dashboards

Wallet-connect flows now double as portfolio trackers. Expect more NFT platforms to fold analytics directly into the connect experience instead of sending users to a separate tab.

RISK

Always verify the contract

Fake collections mirroring popular names remain the top scam vector. Check the verified contract address before bidding or minting — never trust a link from an unsolicited DM.
