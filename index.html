How to use Apptochain

About 10 minutes to set up

  1. Connect your wallet

    Press Connect wallet at the top. Pick MetaMask, Trust, Binance Wallet or another browser wallet and approve the connection. Apptochain only reads data; it never asks you to sign or approve spending. No browser wallet? Choose "Watch an address" and paste any wallet address.

  2. Set up your project

    Open Settings. Enter your project name, token symbol, chain and token contract address. Add your monthly spend so Apptochain can work out your treasury runway. With a wallet connected, press "Read from chain" to fill in total supply.

    Open Settings

  3. Add your liquidity pools

    In Liquidity, press + Add pool. Enter the pair, the DEX, total liquidity in USD, and how much of the LP is locked. Apptochain shows the price impact of $1k to $250k sells and flags unlocked LP.

    Open Liquidity

  4. Add treasury wallets and sync them

    In Treasury, add each wallet with its full 0x address, type and USD value. Press Sync on-chain to read each wallet's native balance (BNB, ETH) and your token balance straight from the blockchain through your connected wallet.

    Open Treasury

  5. Add vesting schedules

    In Vesting, add each group (team, investors, advisors) with total tokens, tokens unlocked so far, and the next unlock date and amount. Progress bars and countdowns update automatically.

    Open Vesting

  6. Set your alert rules

    In Alerts, turn rules on or off and set your own limits, such as "warn me when more than 25% of LP is unlocked" or "when an unlock is within 30 days". Triggered alerts show on the Overview and as a red count in the menu.

    Open Alerts

  7. Run the AI agents

    In AI Agents, pick Research, Portfolio or Operations, add any notes, and press Run agent. The first time, you'll be asked to allow the AI.Reports are saved to your history, and the Operations agent drafts a community update you can copy to X or Telegram.

    Open AI Agents

Questions about Apptochain

What is Apptochain?

Apptochain is a token management and liquidity monitoring platform for Web3 project teams. It shows your liquidity pools, treasury wallets and vesting schedules in one dashboard, alerts you when something changes, and uses AI agents to explain your data in plain language.

Who is Apptochain for?

Token projects from launch to maturity, launchpads and incubators that support many projects, market makers, and funds that manage crypto treasuries.

Which blockchains does it support?

Apptochain launches on BNB Chain. Base and Ethereum follow in Phase 3 of the roadmap, then more EVM chains in Phase 4.

Does Apptochain have a token?

No. Apptochain is a software product paid for by subscription. Be careful of anyone offering an "Apptochain token": it is not ours. Official news comes only from our X and Telegram channels.

How much does it cost?

There is a free Starter plan for one token and one pool. Paid plans add real-time alerts, more pools and wallets, the AI agents and public transparency pages. Partner plans cover launchpads and incubators with many projects. Final prices are announced at launch.

Can Apptochain move my funds?

No. Apptochain only reads public blockchain data through your wallet. It never asks for a signature, a transaction or a spending approval, and it never holds your private keys.

Is my project data private?

Yes. Your workspace is private to you. Only data you choose to publish, such as a future public transparency page, can be seen by others.

Why can't it find my wallet?

Browser wallets connect when Apptochain is opened in a desktop browser with the wallet extension installed, or inside a wallet app's built-in browser. Anywhere else, use "Watch an address" and enter balances by hand.

Where do the pool numbers come from?

For now you enter pool liquidity and lock status, and Apptochain calculates price impact, LP-locked share, runway, health score and alerts from them. Automatic pool tracking is part of the Foundation phase on the roadmap.

Are the AI reports financial advice?

No. The AI agents give information to help your team understand its data. They never trade or move funds. Check important figures before acting on them or publishing them.

How do I get support or follow updates?

Follow Apptochain on X and join the Telegram channel using the links in the menu. Project teams that want early access can apply there.
