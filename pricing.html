Payment questions

How crypto payments work on AppToChain.

How does crypto payment work?▼
Click "Pay with Crypto", choose your preferred network and token, then send the exact amount to our wallet address. Your transaction is automatically verified on-chain — usually within 30–60 seconds. No manual confirmation needed.
Which networks can I pay on?▼
You can pay on: Ethereum (ETH/USDT/USDC), BNB Chain (BNB/USDT), Tron (TRX/USDT), Polygon (USDC), Base (ETH/USDC), and Arbitrum (ETH). Gas fees are cheapest on Tron and Polygon.
How fast is activation?▼
Instant. The moment your transaction gets 1 confirmation on-chain, our system verifies it automatically and upgrades your account. Tron is fastest (~3 seconds), Ethereum is slowest (~15–30 seconds).
What if I send from an exchange?▼
You can send from any exchange (Binance, Bybit, OKX, etc.) or any wallet. After sending, paste your transaction hash in the "Manual Verify" field and we'll verify and activate instantly.
Are payments refundable?▼
Blockchain transactions are irreversible. If you have an issue, email hello@apptochain.xyz and we'll resolve it fairly. We do not issue refunds after Pro features have been used.
Does Pro auto-renew?▼
No auto-renewal. Each payment gives you 30 days (monthly) or 365 days (annual). You'll get an email reminder before expiry. Renew when you want to continue — no surprise charges.
